08 · Explore the evidence

Every interpretation
has an evidence trail.

Behind these cases is a formal evidence model: an ontology whose rules a computer checks. It traces every statement to its source, keeps apart things that are easy to conflate, and lets a promising interpretation stay on hold with the reason in view.

What the evidence model will not allow

  • A marker gene or RNA signal read as a methane flux
  • A sequencing library treated as a physical sample
  • An unassayed gene reported as absent
  • A concentration relabeled as a flux
  • A hypothesis promoted to a fact
  • A carbon-credit approval asserted by the graph

For a project developer or verifier, the output is an evidence-readiness dossier, not a credit or a tonnage estimate. It shows which molecular evidence can answer which review question, and which measurement would resolve what it cannot.

Molecular Verification Ontology 0.2.0, built on the W3C RDF, OWL and SHACL standards. Each of its 18 fixed review queries was checked against the source tables. This public view shows three curated cases; the internal model holds selected evidence for 145 genome records and has not yet had independent scientific review.
